# Berlin – Places and Neighbourhoods

## Mitte

### Brandenburg Gate
- Type: Landmark
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Tags: history, iconic, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 30–60 minutes
- Summary: One of Berlin’s most famous landmarks and a symbol of German reunification. Built in the 18th century, it once stood between East and West Berlin during the Cold War. Best visited early morning or late evening to avoid crowds.

### Museum Island
- Type: Museum cluster
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Tags: museums, culture, indoor
- Typical visit time: 3–5 hours
- Summary: A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring five major museums, including the Pergamon Museum and Neues Museum. Ideal for art and history lovers; plan ahead as visiting all museums takes several hours.

### Alexanderplatz
- Type: Square / transport hub
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Tags: urban, shopping, transport
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A busy central square known for its shops, restaurants, and transport connections. It’s home to the TV Tower (Fernsehturm), offering panoramic views over Berlin.

### Berlin Cathedral (Berliner Dom)
- Type: Church / landmark
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Tags: history, architecture, indoor
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A grand cathedral with impressive architecture and a dome you can climb for city views.

## Kreuzberg

### Görlitzer Park
- Type: Park
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Tags: outdoor, local, relaxed
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A popular park among locals, especially in summer. Known for its laid-back atmosphere, street food vendors, and multicultural vibe.

### Markthalle Neun
- Type: Food market
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Tags: food, indoor, local
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A historic market hall offering local and international food. Street Food Thursday is especially popular, with many small vendors and a lively atmosphere.

### Landwehr Canal
- Type: Canal / walking area
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Tags: walking, scenic, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A scenic canal perfect for walking or relaxing by the water. The area around Admiralbrücke is especially lively in the evenings.

### Viktoriapark
- Type: Park
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Tags: nature, viewpoint, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A hilltop park with a waterfall and great views over the city. Less crowded than other parks.

---

## Prenzlauer Berg

### Mauerpark
- Type: Park / flea market
- Neighbourhood: Prenzlauer Berg
- Tags: outdoor, market, music
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: Famous for its Sunday flea market and open-air karaoke. A lively spot with street performers, food stalls, and a strong local community feel.

### Kollwitzplatz
- Type: Square
- Neighbourhood: Prenzlauer Berg
- Tags: local, food, relaxed
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A charming square surrounded by cafés and boutiques. Known for its weekly farmers market and relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere.

### Kulturbrauerei
- Type: Cultural complex
- Neighbourhood: Prenzlauer Berg
- Tags: culture, nightlife, history
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: A former brewery turned into a cultural hub with museums, cinemas, and event spaces. Hosts exhibitions and nightlife events throughout the year.

---

## Friedrichshain

### East Side Gallery
- Type: Street art / wall
- Neighbourhood: Friedrichshain
- Tags: history, street art, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: The longest remaining section of the Berlin Wall, covered in murals by international artists. A powerful open-air gallery reflecting political change and freedom.

### Boxhagener Platz
- Type: Square / market
- Neighbourhood: Friedrichshain
- Tags: local, market, relaxed
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A popular local square with a weekly market and many nearby cafés and bars. A great place to experience the neighborhood vibe.

### RAW-Gelände
- Type: Cultural / nightlife area
- Neighbourhood: Friedrichshain
- Tags: nightlife, alternative, culture
- Typical visit time: 2–4 hours
- Summary: An alternative cultural space with clubs, bars, street art, and event venues. Known for its edgy atmosphere and nightlife scene.

### Oberbaum Bridge
- Type: Bridge / landmark
- Neighbourhood: Friedrichshain
- Tags: architecture, history, scenic
- Typical visit time: 30–60 minutes
- Summary: One of Berlin’s most iconic bridges connecting Friedrichshain and Kreuzberg, especially beautiful at sunset.

---

## Neukölln

### Tempelhofer Feld
- Type: Park / open space
- Neighbourhood: Neukölln
- Tags: outdoor, unique, local
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: A vast former airport turned into a public park where locals cycle, skate, picnic, and relax. Its wide open runways make it a unique urban space, especially popular on sunny days.

### Weserkiez
- Type: Neighbourhood area
- Neighbourhood: Neukölln
- Tags: nightlife, local, trendy
- Typical visit time: 2–4 hours
- Summary: A lively area filled with bars, cafés, and restaurants. Known for its alternative and international vibe, especially in the evenings.

### Körnerpark
- Type: Park
- Neighbourhood: Neukölln
- Tags: quiet, scenic, local
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A small but beautifully designed park with a more formal layout, fountains, and a peaceful atmosphere compared to other parks in Berlin.

### Klunkerkranich
- Type: Rooftop bar / viewpoint
- Neighbourhood: Neukölln
- Tags: nightlife, view, alternative
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: A rooftop bar on top of a shopping center offering great sunset views and a relaxed atmosphere.

---

## Charlottenburg

### Charlottenburg Palace
- Type: Palace / museum
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Tags: history, culture, indoor
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: The largest palace in Berlin, featuring baroque architecture, beautiful gardens, and royal interiors. A great place to explore Prussian history.

### Kurfürstendamm (Ku’damm)
- Type: Shopping street
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Tags: shopping, urban, upscale
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: One of Berlin’s most famous shopping streets, lined with international brands, cafés, and restaurants. Ideal for a relaxed stroll or shopping.

### Savignyplatz
- Type: Square
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Tags: cafés, relaxed, local
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A charming square surrounded by restaurants, bookstores, and cafés. Popular with locals for a quieter and more refined atmosphere.

---

## Tiergarten

### Tiergarten Park
- Type: Park
- Neighbourhood: Tiergarten
- Tags: nature, central, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 2–3 hours
- Summary: Berlin’s largest central park, ideal for walking, cycling, or relaxing. Located between major landmarks, it offers a green escape in the middle of the city.

### Victory Column (Siegessäule)
- Type: Monument
- Neighbourhood: Tiergarten
- Tags: history, viewpoint, outdoor
- Typical visit time: 45–60 minutes
- Summary: A historic monument with a viewing platform offering panoramic views over Berlin. Located in the center of Tiergarten.

---

## Schöneberg

### Rathaus Schöneberg
- Type: Historic building
- Neighbourhood: Schöneberg
- Tags: history, political, landmark
- Typical visit time: 30–60 minutes
- Summary: Famous for John F. Kennedy’s “Ich bin ein Berliner” speech. A symbolic place in Cold War history.

### Akazienkiez
- Type: Neighbourhood area
- Neighbourhood: Schöneberg
- Tags: local, cafés, relaxed
- Typical visit time: 1–2 hours
- Summary: A charming neighborhood with independent shops, cafés, and a relaxed local atmosphere.

## Wedding

- Neighbourhood: Wedding
- Tags: nature, swimming, outdoor

- Summary: A popular lake for swimming in summer. Offers a more local and less touristy experience.

### Silent Green Kulturquartier

- Neighbourhood: Wedding
- Tags: culture, events, alternative
