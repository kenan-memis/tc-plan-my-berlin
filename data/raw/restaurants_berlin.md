# Berlin – Restaurants

## Mitte

### Monsieur Vuong
- Type: Sit-down
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Cuisine: Vietnamese
- Price level: $$
- Tags: casual, popular, quick
- Summary: A well-known Vietnamese restaurant with fresh, flavorful dishes. Often busy, but service is fast and efficient.

### Zur letzten Instanz
- Type: Sit-down
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Cuisine: German
- Price level: $$
- Tags: traditional, historic, cosy
- Summary: Berlin’s oldest restaurant, serving classic German dishes in a historic setting. Great for experiencing traditional cuisine.

### House of Small Wonder
- Type: Café / brunch
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Cuisine: International
- Price level: $$
- Tags: brunch, trendy, cosy
- Summary: A stylish café known for its brunch menu and unique interior design. Popular with both locals and tourists.

### Curry 61
- Type: Street food
- Neighbourhood: Mitte
- Cuisine: German / currywurst
- Price level: $
- Tags: quick, casual, local
- Summary: A popular currywurst spot near Hackescher Markt. Fast, affordable, and a classic Berlin street food experience.

## Kreuzberg

### Mustafa’s Gemüse Kebap
- Type: Street food
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Cuisine: Turkish / döner
- Price level: $
- Tags: quick, popular, casual
- Summary: One of Berlin’s most famous döner spots. Expect long queues, but the food is widely considered worth the wait.

### Burgermeister
- Type: Street food
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Cuisine: Burgers
- Price level: $
- Tags: quick, casual, popular
- Summary: A legendary burger spot located in a former public toilet under the U-Bahn tracks. Known for simple but high-quality burgers.

### Café am Engelbecken
- Type: Café
- Neighbourhood: Kreuzberg
- Cuisine: German / café
- Price level: $$
- Tags: relaxed, scenic, brunch
- Summary: A peaceful café by the water, perfect for breakfast or a relaxed coffee break.

## Charlottenburg

### House of Small Wonder (Charlottenburg)
- Type: Café / brunch
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Cuisine: International
- Price level: $$
- Tags: brunch, stylish, cosy
- Summary: A beautifully designed café offering brunch and light meals. Known for its calm atmosphere and aesthetic interior.

### Diener Tattersall
- Type: Sit-down
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Cuisine: European
- Price level: $$$
- Tags: fine dining, modern, dinner
- Summary: A modern European restaurant with seasonal ingredients and elegant presentation. Ideal for a special evening.

### Curry 36 (Zoo area)
- Type: Street food
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Cuisine: German / currywurst
- Price level: $
- Tags: quick, casual, classic
- Summary: A famous spot for currywurst near the Zoo area. Fast, simple, and a staple of Berlin street food culture.

### Mustafa’s Gemüse Kebap (Zoo area)
- Type: Street food
- Neighbourhood: Charlottenburg
- Cuisine: Turkish / döner
- Price level: $
- Tags: quick, popular, casual
- Summary: A branch of the famous döner chain near the Zoo area. Expect fresh ingredients and a busy but fast-moving line.
