# Berlin – Transport Overview

## Zones

- Berlin’s public transport is divided into zones **A**, **B**, and **C**.
- Zone A covers the city centre inside the S-Bahn ring.
- Zone B covers the outer parts of the city.
- Zone C includes surrounding areas such as Potsdam and the airport region.

## Ticket types

- Single ticket (AB): Valid for around 120 minutes and allows transfers within zones A and B.
- Day ticket (AB): Valid until 3 am the following day with unlimited travel.
- Short trip ticket: Valid for a limited number of stops (useful for very short distances).
- Recommended for visitors: Day ticket AB or multi-day tourist passes depending on length of stay.

## Main lines / hubs (high-level)

### Alexanderplatz
A major transport hub connecting multiple S-Bahn, U-Bahn, tram, and bus lines. Very central and useful for reaching many parts of the city.

### Berlin Hauptbahnhof
The main train station with connections to regional and long-distance trains, as well as S-Bahn and U-Bahn lines.

### Zoologischer Garten
An important station in the western part of the city with S-Bahn, U-Bahn, and bus connections. Close to shopping areas and the zoo.

### S-Bahn Ring (S41 / S42)
A circular train line that loops around central Berlin, connecting many key districts including Friedrichshain, Kreuzberg, and Prenzlauer Berg. Very useful for navigating between neighborhoods.
